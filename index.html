Pilot v2 · rewritten to Akshay's brief

10 drafts following the Akshay-authored spec — US-first compliance angle, exactly one US-side term, "two disconnected advisors" USP, CA Co-Founder only (no CPA, no CGO).

What's different from v1 (the 3,221 already drafted)
USP "Two disconnected advisors blind spot" — Indian CA + US CPA who never speak. We're the only firm under one roof. Replaces the geography-based framing.
ORDER US-side compliance issue named first. India is the differentiator ("what a US-only CPA would miss"). v1 led with founder geography.
TERM Exactly ONE US-side term per email. v1 stacked 4-5 (Section 174 + transfer pricing + FEMA + GST + state nexus). One = credibility; multiple = template.
TITLE Akshay = "CA, Co-Founder, USAIndiaCFO". Never CPA. Never CGO. CA only. Always exactly once per body.
CTA Specific 20-minute call with Akshay. v1 was generic "lighten the compliance load" — softer but less actionable.
OPEN Opens with company's specific business detail. v1 opened with "Took a quick look at..." (implicit I) — Akshay's rule forbids any I/We/Hope/firm-name opener.
SIG New "Scheduling a call with: Akshay Nahar | CA · Co-Founder" line. Pre-frames principal on the call without taking body word-count.
WORDS Body 95-135 words, 4 paragraphs (Hook → Insight → Introduction → CTA). v1 was 78-146 with looser structure.

Pipeline flow
Five stages from CSV to staged-for-send. Re-runnable on any future CSV.
  1. 01 · INPUT
    CSV + CRM dump
    Apollo export + Zoho official backup.
  2. 02 · DEDUP
    3-key match
    Email · phone · name+company.
  3. 03 · SCORE
    ICP · EU exclude
    Title × size × industry × cross-border → tier A/B/C/D. EU rows holdback.
  4. 04 · DRAFT
    Research-targeted
    WebFetch each company site → unique hook → Akshay-only body.
  5. 05 · STAGE
    Apollo Sequences
    Tranches by per-mailbox daily cap. Never auto-send.
