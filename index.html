Outreach pool by ICP tier
All 8,277 candidates were scored 1–10 on title × company size × industry fit × cross-border signal, then bucketed into A (8–10), B (5–7), C (3–4), D (1–2).
Why each tier matters
Tranches by tier so partner can stage sends. Tier A is the first push; B follows after pilot reply data; C/D get nurture sequences.
Tier A · 3,628
ICP 8–10. Founder/CEO/CFO at India-rooted firms with US revenue. First send tranche.
Tier B · 2,722
ICP 5–7. Senior but not C-suite, or smaller companies. Standard pool, send after Tier A pilot data.
Tier C · 520
ICP 3–4. Lower fit; nurture sequence, no rep-driven first-touch.
Tier D · 74
ICP 1–2. Hold queue; weekly review only.
EU holdback · 18
GDPR/UK GDPR — work-email TLD or country signals EU/EEA/UK. Excluded from all tiers.

Pipeline flow
Five stages from CSV to staged-for-send. Re-runnable on any future CSV.
  1. 01 · INPUT
    CSV + CRM dump
    Apollo export + Zoho official backup.
  2. 02 · DEDUP
    3-key match
    Email · phone · name+company.
  3. 03 · SCORE
    ICP · EU exclude
    Title × size × industry × cross-border → tier A/B/C/D. EU rows holdback.
  4. 04 · DRAFT
    Research-targeted
    WebFetch each company site → unique hook → Akshay-only body.
  5. 05 · STAGE
    Apollo Sequences
    Tranches by per-mailbox daily cap. Never auto-send.
